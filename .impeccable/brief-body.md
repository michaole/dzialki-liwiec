Scope: site/index.html — the whole static site (one screen). Mode: operate.
Audience/job: the owner, on a laptop, a few minutes after the nightly email: see what is new or cheaper since the last visit, open a couple of listings, shortlist, jot notes. Occasional comparison of everything still on the market.
Content: every plot in the Liwiec places list across 4 portals; price, PLN/m², area, village, river section, source, days on market, price history, gone listings.
Constraints: dense (no "too sparse"), no gimmicks, Polish UI, favourites + notes stored in the browser, data refreshed nightly.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Price is the loudest thing on every row, and whatever changed since the last visit is shouted on fluorescent card. Refuses the category default of photo cards and KPI tiles; this is one dense, sortable price list.
OWN-WORLD: concrete-grey ground, black felt-tip ink, kraft board for neutral chrome, fluorescent lime (NOWE) and fluorescent orange (TANIEJ) card tags stuck slightly askew; prices and tags in a heavy condensed face with tabular figures; everything else a quiet system sans.
STORY: the visitor sees how many plots are new or cheaper since last time, scans prices down one right-aligned column, opens or stars a few, leaves a note, leaves.
FIRST VIEWPORT: slim ink header (name, data date, counts "od ostatniej wizyty": nowe / tańsze / zniknęło as clickable fluo tags), one filter row, then the table filling the rest: star, state tag, village + section, title link, PRICE (largest type on screen, right-aligned), PLN/m², area, days-on-market ramp, source, note. Raises: right-aligned tabular figures tight to the column edge (j-card); one fluo accent per live state, never decoration (metro); days on market on one fixed fresh-to-stale ramp (star atlas); columns never shift, filters only hide rows (Crouwel). Signature move: the askew fluo price card on changed rows, carrying old→new price.
FORM: Targ — fluorescent market price cards; position 4 on the ordered list; seed key b4ffa6fd.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
